=== WP Job Portal changelog ===

== Changelog ==

= 2.8.1 =
Security.

* Security: fixed an SQL injection reachable by a logged-in job seeker or employer through crafted field names in the profile and resume forms. Saved data is now written only to the plugin's own database columns. All sites should update.
* Security: fixed a reflected cross-site scripting issue in the e-mail form on an employer's list of applicants. A crafted link could run script when a signed-in user pressed Send. The request now needs the page's security token, real resume and job ids, and the job's owner or an administrator. All sites should update.

= 2.8.0 =
Major update: a redesigned admin, a clearer hiring flow and security fixes. Take a backup before updating: changes to existing records wait until an administrator confirms there is one.

* Security: resume files and custom-field uploads are stored under random names in folders that refuse direct requests (Apache and IIS; see the FAQ for nginx), and are handed out only through the permission-checked download. Files uploaded before 2.8.0 are protected on the first page load after updating. All sites should update.
* Security: stricter permission checks on application and job-apply requests, and on which requests visitors, job seekers and employers may send.
* Security: the resume page no longer prints values sent by the browser.
* Security: an employer can open a resume that is not searchable only when their job received it or they unlocked it, and its contact details follow the same rule (Patchstack report 38700).
* Security: the privacy eraser now also deletes the person's uploaded files and job alerts.
* Includes the 2.7.1 and 2.7.2 security fixes.
* New: redesigned admin with one menu, shared list screens, an Approvals page, menu search (Ctrl K), a notifications bell and an Applications list.
* New: a dashboard that shows what needs doing, with trends, a date range and a layout each administrator can arrange.
* New: hiring status is separate from approval; candidates can withdraw an application, and candidate emails can be switched on or off per status.
* New: employers can close a job early and are reminded before it expires; "Closed to New Applicants" now closes the job on the site.
* New: first steps for new employers and job seekers.
* New: a saved search can email new matching jobs; a job alert no longer needs a category.
* New: clean addresses for jobs, companies and resumes, and JobPosting structured data, meta and Open Graph tags on job pages.
* New: a warning when emails fail and a Send a test email button; default emails use the site's name and include one-click unsubscribe.
* Changed: a job needs a city or a remote workplace before it is saved.
* Changed: job seekers save jobs; "Shortlisted" is now only an application status.
* Changed: maps use the site's own Google Maps key; there is no shared key.
* Changed: deleting the plugin keeps your data unless you choose otherwise. Tables move to utf8mb4 and exact money amounts in the background after the update.
* Improved: job search matches every word typed, and a typed city counts.
* Improved: faster pages: fewer database queries on job lists, minified stylesheets, and job alerts sent in batches.
* Improved: plainer wording and better keyboard and screen-reader support on admin screens.
* Fixed: total experience counts a current job up to today.
* Fixed: a job that fails to save keeps what was typed and says why.
* Fixed: saving settings without changing anything no longer changes them.
* Fixed: a site using OpenStreetMap had its maps hidden when it had no Google Maps key; only Google maps are hidden now, and a job's empty Map section goes with them.
* Fixed: a resume a visitor made while applying is private even with Searchable ticked: it is not in resume search, the resume widgets and shortcodes, the resume RSS feed or category counts, and only the employers whose job received it can open it. This applies to every resume without an owner, including one an administrator made without choosing a user.
* Fixed: "how long ago" labels follow each language's word order ("vor 3 Tagen", not "3 Tage vor").
* Fixed: the resume page heads the address section "Address" in every language, not "Add Address".
* Fixed: Licences and add-ons have their own admin menu item again, with the number of updates waiting; Reports and Shortcodes are top-level items again too.
* Fixed: the WordPress menu lists Candidates (with new applications) and Companies again, and stays open on every plugin screen with the entry for that screen marked.
* Fixed: on WordPress.com and other Jetpack sites, a login from the WP Job Portal login form was sent to a separate math page and then to wp-login.php, where the password had to be typed again. The form now shows Jetpack's math question itself.
* Fixed: an empty date-picker strip showed at the foot of pages with a date field.
* Fixed: More Actions on an applicant opens downward when there is more room below, and no longer hides under the WordPress toolbar.
* Fixed: the minified stylesheet was older than the stylesheet it stands for.
* Fixed: "Email me resume and attachments" now attaches the files.
* Fixed: wrong settings defaults on new installs.
* Fixed: a fatal error when prices use "fit to currency" decimals.
* Fixed: edit pages were titled "Add Resume", "Add Job" or "Add Company" in the browser tab.

= 2.7.2 =
Security.

* Security: fixed a missing permission check on the admin "show role info" request, where any logged-in user could read another user's company name, resume title or name by account id. The request is now restricted to administrators. All sites should update.

= 2.7.1 =
Security and fixes.

* Security: fixed a stored cross-site scripting issue where a logged-in employer could place script in a job's structured data that ran for visitors of the job page. The job's JSON-LD is now encoded so markup cannot break out of it. All sites should update.
* Fixed: the registration page showed a blank area to visitors who were already signed in; it now links them to their dashboard.
* Fixed: the Custom After-Registration Link for employers and job seekers was ignored unless an After-Registration Page was also chosen. It now works on its own.
* Fixed: a required custom field on the registration form could be left empty once an earlier required field was filled.
* Improved: the employer and job seeker registration shortcodes hide the role choice by default, since they already set the account type. Use hide_role="0" to show it.
* Improved: a visitor who doesn't choose employer or job seeker is now told to choose one, alongside the other form errors.
* Improved: clearer descriptions for the registration and after-registration link settings.

= 2.7.0 =
Major update: the separate add-ons become four premium products, and twelve of them are now free.

* New: Recruit Suite, a hiring pipeline for teams with stages, scorecards, shared notes, offers with approvals and e-signatures, and recruiting reports.
* New: Marketplace Suite, to sell job packages, credits and subscriptions through Stripe, PayPal or WooCommerce, with paid featured jobs, companies and resumes.
* New: Smart Search & Matching, with AI job and resume search, suggested jobs and resumes, and match scores.
* New: Pro, with the advanced resume builder, resume search, messaging, PDF and export, reports, instant job alerts and maps.
* New: twelve former add-ons are now free: Shortlist, Tags, Job Alert, RSS, Social Share, Print, Tell a Friend, Copy Job, Cron Job, All Companies, Job Listing Enhancer and Widgets.
* New: four-step setup wizard and sample data that fits your site.
* New: Account Sign-Up setting, separate from WordPress's "Anyone can register".
* New: saved job searches for job seekers.
* Fixed: updating from 2.6 could end on a critical error. Existing job and company links keep working after the update.
* Fixed: the salary, currency, education and postcode filters in search.
* Fixed: RSS feeds, Tags, Shortlisted Jobs and Job Alert on new installs.
* Fixed: other errors on new installs and in the Insufficient Credits popup.
